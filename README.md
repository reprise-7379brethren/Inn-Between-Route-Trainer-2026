## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Inn Between**. This trainer provides convenience tools for The Inn Between's choice-driven visual novel gameplay. It can help manage route progress, relationship values, endings, text presentation, and collectible story content without altering the game's core narrative.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to revisit dialogue choices, explore romance routes, reach alternate endings, unlock story artwork, or reduce repetition during multiple playthroughs.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The Inn Between is a visual novel and does not use conventional character movement or free-roaming controls. Progress is made through dialogue, menus, and story choices.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not feature a conventional inventory, equipment system, or item-based character build. The trainer therefore focuses on narrative variables, route progress, endings, and collectible artwork.

### World & Environment

Myka Riley's story follows his search for missing memories and his lost family after entering a hidden world of gods, ghosts, and mythical creatures. Use the trainer after saving when changing route values or unlocking story content.

### Combat & Weapons

The Inn Between does not use a conventional combat system. Its progression is driven by dialogue, relationships, narrative decisions, and multiple endings rather than battles or character statistics.
